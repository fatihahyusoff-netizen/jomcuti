Put your destination and package photos in this folder.

Example: save a Langkawi photo as langkawi.jpg here, then in content.js set
image: "images/langkawi.jpg" for the Langkawi destination.

Until you add a photo, the site automatically shows a colourful icon
circle instead, so it's safe to publish before your photos are ready.

See README.md in the main folder for full instructions.
